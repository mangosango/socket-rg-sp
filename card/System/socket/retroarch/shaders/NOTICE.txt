slot-lcd.glsl adapts the LCD3x and grid effects from Slot:
https://github.com/BrandonKowalski/slot, crates/slot-gfx/src/shaders.rs, GAME_FRAG.
The adaptation uses RetroArch texture and viewport uniforms. It does not include
Slot's screen layout, bezel, brightness, or final display color adjustments.

Slot source license: GNU GPL version 3 or later. See COPYING.
LCD3x math: Gigaherz, public domain, as identified in Slot licenses/shaders.txt.
